# ⚙️ CRM-Style Lead Automation System

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is a CRM-style lead automation system designed to capture, organize, and manage leads efficiently using spreadsheet-based tracking and email automation.

It helps businesses respond faster, reduce manual workload, and improve lead conversion through automated workflows.

---

## 🚨 Problem

Manual lead handling using spreadsheets and email often leads to:

- Slow response times
- Missed follow-ups
- Disorganized lead data
- Lost sales opportunities

---

## 💡 Solution

This automation system solves those problems by:

- Automatically capturing incoming leads
- Storing and organizing data in Google Sheets
- Sending instant email responses to leads
- Categorizing leads based on priority
- Tracking lead status in one centralized system

---

## ⚙️ Tools Used

- Google Sheets (Database / CRM-like tracking)
- Zapier (Automation workflows)
- Gmail (Email automation)
- CRM logic (Lead categorization & workflow design)

---

## 🔄 Workflow

1. A lead submits a form or inquiry
2. Zapier automatically captures the data
3. Lead information is stored in Google Sheets
4. System sends an instant email response to the lead
5. Leads are categorized (e.g., High, Medium, Low priority)
6. High-priority leads trigger notifications to the business owner
7. All leads are tracked for follow-ups and status updates

---

## 🧠 Key Features

- Automated lead capture system
- Instant email response automation
- Lead prioritization logic (Hot / Warm / Cold)
- Centralized lead database in Google Sheets
- Reduced manual workload for businesses

---

## 📊 Results

- Faster lead response time
- Improved lead organization
- Reduced manual data entry
- Better follow-up consistency
- Higher potential conversion rates
